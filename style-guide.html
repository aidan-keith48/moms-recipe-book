Body paragraph — Lora

A little handwritten note might sit here — the kind of line Mom would jot on the back of a recipe card, like "double the vanilla if you're feeling fancy."

Ingredients list — Lora
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
Method steps — Lora
  1. Preheat oven to 350°F and line a baking pan.
  2. Whisk melted butter and sugar until glossy.
  3. Beat in eggs one at a time, then fold in dry ingredients.
  4. Bake 25–30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out mostly clean.
