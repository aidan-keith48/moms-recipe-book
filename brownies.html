Brownies

SERVES 12

Ingredients

  • 300g butter
  • 400g chocolate
  • 350g sugar
  • 5–6 eggs
  • 1 tbsp vanilla
  • 150g flour
  • 90g walnuts
  • Pinch of salt
Oven
180°C · 20–25 min

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
  2. Add the chocolate and stir until fully combined, then remove from heat.
  3. Add the sugar and stir.
  4. Transfer the mixture to a bowl and let it cool slightly.
  5. Add the eggs and stir.
  6. Fold in the flour and a pinch of salt.
  7. Fold in the walnuts.
  8. Pour the batter into a baking pan.
  9. Bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes.
It's ready when a toothpick comes out almost clean.
Mom's Tip:

You can melt the butter and chocolate together in the microwave in short bursts, stirring each time.
